Common questions

How do I blur a face?

Open a video, then tap Blur all faces to cover everyone automatically, or tap Add blur to place one by hand.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your phone. Your videos are never uploaded, copied, or sent to us or anyone else.

A face was missed. What do I do?

Tap Add blur, drop it on the spot, and use the arrow to track it as the person moves.

How do I save my edited video?

Tap Export. A clean copy is saved to your photo library.

How do I cover a face a different way?

Select a blur, open Style, and choose blur, pixels, blackout, or an emoji. You can pick what sits behind an emoji too.
