BleepFace

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

BleepFace is built so your photos and videos are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. BleepFace does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There are no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Your photos and videos

The videos you open, record, edit, check, or export in BleepFace stay on your device. Face detection, tracking, blurring, and the Check attack all run locally using on device tools. We never upload, copy, or send your videos, frames from them, or any face data to us or to anyone else.

Photo library access

BleepFace asks for access to the videos you choose so you can open them, and for permission to add your exported videos back to your library. It only sees the items you pick. It does not browse, read, or modify the rest of your library, and it never deletes anything.

Camera and microphone

Shoot uses the camera and microphone to record video with faces covered live. Recordings are saved only to your device and only when you choose to keep them. Nothing from the camera is transmitted anywhere.

Speech recognition

If you use audio bleeping, BleepFace transcribes the audio of your video on your device, using Apple's on device speech recognition, to find words you may want to bleep. The audio and the transcript never leave your device.

Check

Check shows you what an attacker could recover from a blur. Everything it shows is generated on your device from footage you already have. Nothing on the Check screen can be exported, saved, or shared, and the screen is excluded from screenshots and screen recording.

Purchases

BleepFace Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and provides us no personal information beyond what is needed to confirm a purchase on your device. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Diagnostics

If you tap Send diagnostics, the app prepares a local log of app events that you can choose to share with us so we can fix problems. File paths and identifiers are removed. The log never contains your video, any frame from it, or any face data. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and it is sent only through your own email or sharing app.

Third parties

BleepFace uses no third party analytics, advertising, or tracking, and includes no third party SDKs that collect data.

Children

BleepFace is a general utility and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
