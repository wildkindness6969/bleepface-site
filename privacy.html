Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

BleepFace is built so your videos never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. BleepFace does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We have no accounts, no sign in, and no analytics.

Your videos

The videos you open and edit in BleepFace stay on your device. They are processed entirely on your phone. We never upload, copy, or send your videos or any frames from them to us or to anyone else. Face detection and blurring run locally using on device tools.

Photo library access

BleepFace asks for access to the videos you choose so you can open them and save your edited result back to your library. It only ever sees the items you pick. It does not browse or read the rest of your library.

Diagnostics

If you tap Send diagnostics, the app prepares a local log of app activity that you can choose to share with us so we can fix problems. The log contains app events only, with file paths and identifiers removed. It never contains your video content or any frames. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Third parties

BleepFace does not use third party analytics, advertising, or tracking. There are no third party SDKs that collect your data.

Children

BleepFace is a general utility and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@bleepface.xyz.
