Support

Found a bug, hit a wall, or have an idea? Every message gets read by the person who built the app.

Email support@bleepface.xyz

If something went wrong, open Tools in the app and tap Send diagnostics. It prepares a short log of app activity you can attach to your email. The log never contains your video or any frame from it, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Common questions

How do I blur a face?

Open a video and tap Blur all faces. BleepFace scans the whole clip, covers every face it finds, and follows them as they move. To cover something the scan missed, tap Add blur and drop it on the spot.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos and videos are processed on your iPhone. There is no server, no account, and no analytics. The app only ever sees the videos you pick.

A face was missed. What do I do?

Scrub to the moment, tap Add blur, and place it. Use Timing to set where it starts and ends, and Scale to make it bigger or stretch it into a rectangle for plates and screens.

How do I change how a blur looks?

Select the blur and open Style. Choose blur, pixels, scramble, blackout, or flare, or drop an emoji on top with a cover underneath. The feather slider sets the edge from hard to soft.

What is Check?

Check runs the same sharpening tools someone would use to reverse a blur and shows you what comes back. If a face is recovered, that blur is not safe and you should use a stronger one or Blackout. Check reports findings only. It will never tell you a video is safe, because no software honestly can. Nothing on the Check screen can be saved or shared.

How do I record with the blur already on?

Tap Shoot. Faces are covered live in the camera as you record, and a final sweep covers anything the live pass missed before the clip is saved. Play the take back and check it yourself before you share it.

How do I save my edited video?

Tap Export. A new copy is saved to your photo library. Your original is never changed.

Why does my export have a BleepFace mark on it?

Free exports carry a small mark. BleepFace Pro removes it. Check and every editing feature stay free.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they come back on any iPhone signed in with the same account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BleepFace. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Newer phones scan faster.
